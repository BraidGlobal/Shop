Shipping Policy

Braid Shipping Policy

Thank you for shopping with Braid! We are committed to getting your order to you as quickly and safely as possible.

Processing Time

Orders are processed within 3–5 business days after payment is received. Orders are not processed, shipped, or delivered on weekends or holidays.

During high-volume periods, special promotions, product launches, or holidays, processing times may be slightly longer.

Shipping Rates & Delivery Estimates

Shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on your location and selected shipping method.

Estimated delivery times:

  • Standard Shipping: 3–7 business days
  • Expedited Shipping: 1–3 business days (if available)

Please note that delivery times are estimates and are not guaranteed.

Order Tracking

Once your order has shipped, you will receive a confirmation email with tracking information. Please allow up to 24 hours for tracking information to update.

Incorrect Shipping Information

Customers are responsible for providing accurate shipping information at checkout. Braid is not responsible for orders shipped to an incorrect address provided by the customer.

If you notice an error in your shipping information, please contact us immediately. We cannot guarantee changes can be made after an order has been processed.

Lost or Delayed Packages

Braid is not responsible for shipping carrier delays caused by weather, holidays, or other circumstances beyond our control.

If your package appears lost, please contact the shipping carrier first. If additional assistance is needed, contact our support team and we will do our best to help.

Damaged Packages

If your order arrives damaged, please contact us within 7 days of delivery and include photos of the damaged item and packaging so we can assist with a replacement or resolution.

Contact Us

For shipping questions, please contact us at contact@braidtech.org
